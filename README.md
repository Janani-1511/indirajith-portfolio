# INDIRAJITH T B — Personal Portfolio Website

A **High-Tech Cyber Dark & Glowing Neon** portfolio for **INDIRAJITH T B**, an Information Technology undergraduate at Dr. N.G.P Institute of Technology, software developer, and AI & IoT enthusiast.

## Design Identity & Palette
- **Background**: Deep Void / Midnight Navy (`#070B14`, `#0D1322`)
- **Card Background**: Translucent Obsidian Glass (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.72)`)
- **Primary Text**: Ultra Crisp White (`#F8FAFC`)
- **Neon Cyan**: Electric Cyan (`#00F0FF`) with intense glow
- **Neon Purple**: Electric Violet (`#B026FF`)
- **Neon Green**: Matrix Green (`#00FF9D`)
- **Borders & Wireframes**: Translucent Cyber Wires (`rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.22)`, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`)
- **Typography**: Syne (Bold Futuristic Display), Space Grotesk, JetBrains Mono, & Plus Jakarta Sans

## Folder Structure
- `index.html`: Main cyber dark neon portfolio showcase.
